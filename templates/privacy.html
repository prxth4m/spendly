Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and account credentials. We also collect the expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, in order to provide the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate and improve Spendly — this includes displaying your expense history, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the support channels listed below.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the Service, and these providers only process data as needed to support that infrastructure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the support channels listed within the app.
